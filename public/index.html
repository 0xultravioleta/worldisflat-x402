How a call is paid

Three steps, one signature

  1. 01 · 402

    Get a price

    The endpoint answers 402 Payment Required with one flat price in USDG.

  2. 02 · Sign

    Sign once

    A wallet or an agent signs one EIP-3009 authorization. No gas, no approve, no account.

  3. 03 · Settle

    Settled on chain

    Ultravioleta's x402 facilitator settles it on Robinhood Chain and pays the gas, and the call is served with $WIF back.

Where a hire's money goes

Three steps, every fee written down

  1. 01 · You pay

    … USDG

    to Flat Fee's wallet, in one signature for that exact amount; … USDG if you hold $WIF and name your wallet.

  2. 02 · Flat Fee locks

    … USDG

    of it in Execution Market's escrow on Robinhood Chain, for the worker. Flat Fee keeps … USDG, or … at the holder price.

  3. 03 · The worker gets

    … USDG

    when Flat Fee approves the answer. Execution Market keeps its …, … USDG, as in this payment ↗; Flat Fee adds … $WIF.

What Flat Fee cannot do with your money

  • Charge more than you sign. Your wallet signs one EIP-3009 authorization for the exact price, to Flat Fee's wallet, valid for 5 minutes.
  • Keep a balance of yours. There is no account, no deposit and no approve(): the facilitator settles that one amount, and nothing else.
  • Use a signature twice. Each authorization carries its own nonce: USDG takes it once, and this server serves one authorization once.
  • Move the worker's pay. Once it is locked, only Execution Market's escrow moves it: to the worker when Flat Fee approves the answer, or back to Flat Fee's wallet if nobody delivers by the deadline.

The fee you paid is not refunded, whatever happens to the task.

What the chain shows, and what it does not

      

        

          
Shows

          
    
            
  • Each paid call: the USDG into the merchant, in a transaction the facilitator submitted.
    • 
            
  • Each $WIF transfer out of the merchant, cashback and worker rewards.
    • 
            
  • Each hire's escrow: the worker's pay locked, then released by Execution Market.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Does not

          
    
            
  • Customers: up to the dated read, every payer was a wallet of the team.
    • 
            
  • A person on the two hires: their worker is a test wallet of the team run by a script.
    • 
            
  • Ratings: the identities are on Robinhood Chain; the ratings are not, yet.
    • 
            
  • A judge of the answer: the demo approves any text answer from the worker it assigned, and caps the $WIF it sends per day.
