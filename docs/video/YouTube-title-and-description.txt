TITLE
Flat Fee | x402 + USDG + $WIF on Robinhood Chain | The World is Flat hackathon

DESCRIPTION
Ask an agent or hire a human for a flat USDG fee on Robinhood Chain. Flat Fee uses plain HTTP and x402: get a price, sign an EIP-3009 authorization, and let Ultravioleta's open-source facilitator settle the payment and pay the gas.

Paid calls return 10 $WIF while the daily cashback budget lasts. Hold 100+ $WIF and pay half: 0.05 USDG per question instead of 0.10. Humans who complete hire tasks earn 50 $WIF.

This demo shows a live HTTP 402 quote and a real, previously settled mainnet question: 0.05 USDG from the payer to the merchant, followed by 10 $WIF cashback from the merchant to the payer. Both receipts were independently checked against Robinhood Chain's public RPC and shown in the explorer.

Built by Ultravioleta DAO for The World is Flat hackathon. Open source, MIT.
