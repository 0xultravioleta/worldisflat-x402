    How it works
    
      
      
        
    1. 402. The endpoint answers 402 Payment Required with a price in USDG on Robinhood Chain (eip155:4663).
      2. 
        
    2. Sign. The wallet or agent signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization for exactly that amount. No gas, no approve.
      3. 
        
    3. Settle. Ultravioleta's x402 facilitator verifies it and settles USDG on chain, paying the gas.
      4. 
        
    4. Serve. The agent answers, or the task goes on the board for a human.
      5. 
        
    5. $WIF back. The merchant wallet sends $WIF to the payer. Hold enough $WIF and the next call costs half.
